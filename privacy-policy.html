Privacy Policy for KG Price Calculator

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Introduction

KG Price Calculator is an offline calculator application. It is designed to calculate prices based on weight or quantity.

Information Collection

This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share users' personal information. All calculations are performed locally on the user's device.

Internet and Network Access

The app does not require an internet connection and does not request internet permission.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change.
